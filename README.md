A Conversational AI Governance & Risk Assessment Framework
Conversational AI is increasingly being used to interact directly with customers, particularly in financial services.
Traditional testing often asks: "Did the AI give the right answer?"
The WHY? Test asks a different question: "Can the AI explain itself when the customer asks WHY?"
The framework is designed to assess whether customer-facing conversational AI can remain accurate, transparent, context-aware and appropriately controlled when customers move beyond the expected conversation.
It focuses on six areas: 
WHY?-Can the AI explain?
SOURCE - Can it substantiate what it says?
CONTEXT - Does it understand the conversation?
CHALLENGE - What happens when the customer pushes back?
ESCALATION - Does it know when to stop?
ACCOUNTABILITY - Who owns the outcome?
